About WeProvideLeads

An Ontario lead-generation company built on local websites that already rank in Google — not ad spend, not an open marketplace.

Why we built this

My name is Mark Hurson. I’ve spent over a decade in digital advertising — running campaigns, buying traffic, and watching the lead generation industry take contractors for a ride. The dominant model is broken: agencies charge retainers to run ads that stop the moment you stop paying, and lead brokers sell the same inquiry to four competitors and call it a “lead.” I’ve seen both sides of that equation, and neither works well for a small contractor.

The idea behind WeProvideLeads is simple. When a homeowner or business owner searches “roofer Guelph,” whoever shows up first gets a steady stream of real customers, month after month, without paying for ads. Most contractors don’t have the time or the specialized skill to get there themselves — and the usual alternatives are poor: pay an ad agency for clicks that may never convert, or buy shared leads that four competitors are calling at the same time. So instead of selling clicks or reselling leads, we do the work of getting found, and route the calls — the first 5 free, then to one contractor exclusively once they’ve gone exclusive for that trade.

What we do

The model has three steps.

  • We build the website. Each site covers one trade and one territory — not a general directory.
  • Homeowners and businesses find it. They search, land on a real local site, and call or fill out a form because they’re ready to hire, not just browsing.
  • We route the inquiry. Your first 5 leads are free. After that, every qualifying inquiry from that site goes to you and only you.

New contractors get their first 5 leads free — no cost, no obligation. After that it’s a flat monthly fee, exclusive to you, tiered by trade. See full pricing.

Results you can check

One of our sites, GuelphDumpsterRental.com, went from no meaningful search visibility to the #1 organic Google result for “Guelph dumpster rental,” and is now running at roughly 30 bookings a month for its contractor partner. We publish the proof — a dated ranking screenshot and a redacted booking request — because you should be able to check the work before trusting the pitch.

To be straight with you: results vary by trade, territory, and competition, and that 30-a-month figure is an estimate, not a metered report. We don’t guarantee rankings or lead volume. Read the full case study →

What we’ve built

WeProvideLeads owns and operates dedicated home service websites across Guelph, Waterloo, Cambridge, Kitchener, Hamilton, Niagara Falls, New Hamburg, Stratford, London, and Orangeville — roofing, electrical, HVAC, mold removal, basement waterproofing, junk removal, moving, fencing, drywalling, window replacement, interlocking, hydrovac, commercial cleaning, general contracting, driveway sealing, metal fabrication, epoxy flooring, power washing, home renovation, and more. Each is a standalone local website with unique content, local schema markup, a Google Business Profile, and Google Search Console verification. Several already rank at #1 or on Page 1 for their core search terms, and we publish those rankings openly because transparency is the whole point.

How we’re different

  • We own the lead source. Inquiries come through websites we built and rank — not an ad account running on your card.
  • Never an open directory. We don’t run a bidding marketplace. It’s limited by trade and territory, and we tell you where you stand.
  • Exclusive means exclusive. Reserve a territory and every qualifying inquiry from that site is yours — not “mostly yours.”
  • No term, no penalty. No setup fee, no contract. If it stops working for you, you cancel.

We also build websites for trades

Beyond generating leads, we also design and build professional websites for trades and home-service businesses — fast, modern, conversion-focused sites built to turn visitors into calls. It’s the same web platform that powers our own lead-generation network. If you’d rather own your site outright, check out our web design studio at Overhauled.ai.

Where we operate

We started in Guelph and Wellington County, and have since expanded into Waterloo, Cambridge, Kitchener, Hamilton, Niagara Falls, New Hamburg, Stratford, London, and Orangeville, with more Ontario communities in progress. We deliberately don’t make promises about markets where we haven’t built inventory yet — the proof-based model only works if the sites are real and ranking, so we expand carefully, city by city and trade by trade.
