The Starting Point

When we began working with this local dumpster rental contractor, the business had no dedicated lead-generation website and virtually no visibility in Google for dumpster rental searches in Guelph.

Without a strong local search presence, the contractor was missing customers who were actively looking for a dumpster rental provider in the area.

A Local Website Designed to Generate Bookings

WeProvideLeads built and launched GuelphDumpsterRental.com around the service and location customers were already searching for. The site was designed to do one job well: turn local search traffic into booked jobs for a single contractor.

  • A dedicated local website for dumpster rental in Guelph
  • Clear service and booking information for customers
  • Content aligned with local search intent
  • Direct inquiries routed to one contractor
  • No sharing or reselling of the inquiries

From No Visibility to a Consistent Source of Business

Within a couple of months, GuelphDumpsterRental.com reached the #1 organic Google result for “Guelph dumpster rental.”

The contractor now receives approximately one booking per day through the website — about 30 bookings per month. Booking volume continues to grow week over week as the site’s local presence develops.

These are not shared leads or inquiries sold to several companies. Customers find the website while actively searching for dumpster rentals in Guelph, and each inquiry is sent exclusively to one contractor.
