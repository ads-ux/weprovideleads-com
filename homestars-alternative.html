Why It Matters

The problem isn’t HomeStars — it’s shared leads and upfront cost

HomeStars is a legitimate platform and works for plenty of contractors. The two things contractors tell us they want to avoid are paying regardless of results, and finding out a competitor got the same homeowner’s information at the same time.

💰

You Pay Either Way

A monthly membership is due whether or not any leads turn into jobs. Per-lead charges stack on top. With WeProvideLeads, if a lead doesn’t close, you don’t pay for it — there’s no membership fee sitting in the background.

⏳

The Race to the Phone

When a lead is shared with multiple contractors, the job usually goes to whoever calls back first. That rewards speed over quality and turns every inquiry into a sprint.

🔒

No Real Exclusivity

Even on paid HomeStars plans, the same homeowner request can still go to your direct competitors. There’s no tier where the lead is contractually yours alone.
