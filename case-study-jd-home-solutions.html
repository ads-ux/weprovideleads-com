Who Jake Is

Jake Daoust runs JD Home Solutions across Barrie, Innisfil, Orillia, Angus, Midhurst, Springwater, Oro-Medonte and Muskoka. Permanent LED lighting, seasonal Christmas lights, pressure washing, gutters, windows and lawn care.

The reputation was already there: nearly a hundred five-star Google reviews. What wasn’t there was the plumbing behind it. His Google profile linked to a broken web address. Two dozen reviews sat unanswered. His service descriptions were blank, his listed hours were wrong, and his website ran stock images instead of the work he actually does.

Good contractor. Leaky pipeline.

What We Built

  • The JD Home Solutions website. Rebuilt around Jake’s real job photos, with a page for every service and every town he covers.
  • His Google Business Profile. Fixed the broken website link, corrected his hours and service area, wrote all 15 service descriptions, replied to every unanswered review, and added 25 real job photos and 3 short videos.
  • Barrie Exterior Cleaning. A second brand just for pressure washing, gutter and window cleaning, with its own website, its own Google profile and its own phone line ringing straight through to Jake.
  • Four Christmas light sites. Barrie Christmas Lights, Muskoka Christmas Lights, Orangeville Christmas Lights and Newmarket Christmas Lights went live at the end of October. Each one is built to win the local search for holiday and permanent lighting in its own town, searches a general contractor’s site rarely ranks for.

Every enquiry from every one of those sites goes to one contractor: Jake.

The four Christmas light sites produced 15 of the 44 in their first four weeks. Those are customers who were never going to find Jake through his own site alone.

One of the Christmas light leads was a referral from another home services company carrying three jobs at once: a commercial car wash, a public library tree and a house.

Permanent lighting deserves a callout. Sixteen requests for a high-ticket, install-once product that keeps earning year-round. That’s not a seasonal blip.

What This Number Is, and Isn’t

To be straight with you: 44 is enquiries delivered, not jobs won. Each one is a real person with a name, a phone number and a specific ask, sent to Jake the moment it came in. Jake is tallying which ones turned into booked work now, and we’ll add that here when it’s in.

We’d rather show you a number we can prove than one we’d have to guess at.

Working With Jake

Honestly, Jake has been one of the easiest partners we’ve had.

He’s fast. A Muskoka form came in at 4:52 on a Monday afternoon, and Jake had written back to the homeowner before 7:15 that evening. A permanent lighting enquiry landed one day and he’d already talked to the customer by the next morning. When we forward a lead, the reply is usually two words, “Got it,” and then he goes and does the work.

He sends us real photos of real jobs. He tells us when something’s working and when it isn’t. And he’s built a business people genuinely rave about, which makes our job a lot easier.

When we asked him how the partnership was going, he didn’t hesitate:

“Mark, you have been an absolute beauty. This has been [so] great.”

— Jake Daoust, owner, JD Home Solutions

Why It Worked

  • One brand per search. Someone typing “Christmas lights Barrie” wants a Christmas light company in Barrie. A dedicated site gives Google exactly that, and lands the customer on a page about the one thing they want.
  • Real work, real reviews. No stock photos, no borrowed reviews. Everything a customer sees is Jake’s.
  • Exclusive. Every enquiry goes to one contractor. Nobody is racing four other companies to the same phone number.
  • A contractor who picks up. The best lead in the world dies in a voicemail box. Jake calls back.
