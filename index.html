Common Questions

Questions about our lead generation service

Straight answers for home service contractors in Guelph, Waterloo, Cambridge, and Kitchener.

Are the leads exclusive to me?

On the shared plan — our default, 10% of completed jobs, nothing upfront — leads rotate among a small, vetted pool for your trade and area. If you want every qualifying lead to yourself, go exclusive for a flat monthly fee and you’re the only contractor who receives leads from that site.

What does it cost?

You pay 10% of the completed job — nothing upfront, nothing if it doesn’t close. Leads are shared among a small pool of contractors. Want every lead in your market to yourself? Go exclusive for a flat monthly fee tiered by trade — from $199/month for lower-ticket categories up to $699/month for high-ticket ones. No contracts, no setup fee, cancel any time. See the full breakdown on the pricing page.

What areas do you cover?

Guelph & Wellington County is our home base and covers most trades. Select trades also reach Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Hamilton, Niagara Falls, New Hamburg, Stratford, London, and Orangeville — check your trade’s availability above for exact coverage.

What if my trade is already taken?

We’ll tell you up front whether it’s open. If it’s filled, you can join the waitlist and we’ll reach out when the slot opens.

How is this different from HomeStars or Thumbtack?

Those platforms sell the same lead to several contractors at once. Our shared pool is small and vetted rather than open to anyone, and our exclusive plan sends every qualifying lead to one contractor only. See the full comparison →
