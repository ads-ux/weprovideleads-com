Common Questions

Renting vs. owning: straight answers

For home service contractors in Guelph, Waterloo, Cambridge, Kitchener, and across Ontario.

What is the difference between renting and owning?

Renting: you pay a flat monthly fee and get the calls and inquiries from one of our existing websites. Owning: you pay once and we build a local lead-generation website for your trade and city that belongs to you. Either way it’s a website built to be found by local customers searching for your trade — not a redesign of your business website.

Who owns the website?

If you rent, we own the website and you keep the business it sends you. If you own, the website is yours.

Where do calls and inquiries go?

Straight to you, either way. Phone calls ring your line, and form inquiries and emails come to your inbox. When you rent, that website’s inquiries go to you and no other contractor. When you own, every call, inquiry, and job it generates is yours.

Do I pay monthly or once upfront?

Renting is a flat monthly fee, tiered by trade — no contract, cancel any time. Owning is one upfront price, with no ongoing payments to us. After handover everything runs on your own accounts: your domain renews yearly through your registrar, hosting for a website this size usually fits a free hosting plan, and calls go to your own business number. We go over it all before you commit.

What does it cost?

It depends on your trade and city, so we quote it rather than publish a table. Renting: your first 5 leads are free, then a flat monthly fee — no setup fee. Owning: one upfront price for the build. Either way, you get the number within one business day, before you owe anything.

Does the free trial apply to owning?

No. The first 5 free leads are for renting only. Owning is a build, so it’s quoted and scheduled.

Are rental leads exclusive to me?

Yes. During your 5 free leads and every month after, inquiries from the website you rent go to you and no other contractor — we trial one contractor per website at a time. Exclusivity is included in the monthly fee; there’s no upgrade to buy. It covers the website you rent, not every website in your area.

What areas do you cover?

Guelph & Wellington County is our home base and covers most trades. Select trades also reach Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Hamilton, Niagara Falls, New Hamburg, Stratford, London, and Orangeville — check your trade’s availability above for exact coverage.

What if my trade is already taken?

We’ll tell you up front whether a website for your trade and city is open to rent. If it’s taken, you can join the waitlist and we’ll reach out when it opens — or ask about having us build a website you own.

How is this different from HomeStars or Thumbtack?

Those platforms sell the same lead to several contractors at once. We never send a lead to more than one contractor. Rent one of our websites and its inquiries go to you alone — or own your website outright. See the full comparison →
