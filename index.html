Common Questions

Questions about our lead generation service

Straight answers for home service contractors in Guelph, Waterloo, Cambridge, and Kitchener.

How do the leads work?

We own a website for each trade in Guelph and Wellington County. When a homeowner finds the site and reaches out, that lead goes straight to you — by phone, form, or email.

Are the leads exclusive to me?

Yes. One contractor per trade per area. We don’t resell or share your leads with competitors.

What does it cost?

A small percentage of the completed job, not a flat fee or a per-lead charge. We work with a small number of vetted, exclusive contractors and agree the exact rate individually with each one — request a quote and we’ll be in touch within one business day. No contracts, no setup fee, cancel any time.

What areas do you cover?

Guelph and Wellington County — Fergus, Elora, Rockwood, Erin, Puslinch, and Centre Wellington. Select trades (Moving, Drywalling, Mold Removal, Hydrovac) also cover Kitchener, Waterloo, and Cambridge.

How fast do I start getting leads?

Sometimes within the hour. Our sites already rank in Google and most trades get leads daily — we just point the calls and forms to you, typically within one business day of signing up.

What if my trade is already taken?

We’ll tell you up front whether it’s open. If it’s filled, you can join the waitlist and we’ll reach out when the slot opens.

How is this different from HomeStars or Thumbtack?

They sell the same lead to several contractors at once. We send every lead from our site to one contractor only — you’re not racing anyone else for the callback. See the full comparison →
