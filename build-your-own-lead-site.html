Your Google Business Profile

A Google Business Profile is Google’s free listing that can show your business on Google Maps and in local search. It’s separate from your website. Your website can receive calls and inquiries without one, and a profile doesn’t guarantee leads. In our experience, though, the websites in our network that get the most calls are connected to one, so we recommend it.

How we help: we’ll help you connect the website to your profile, or set one up if you don’t have one yet and your business is eligible. What you do: Google verifies the business itself, so completing verification is your step.

Google’s rules (not ours): one profile per business location, it must use your real business name, and your business must serve customers in person — at your location or theirs.

Source: Google’s guidelines for representing your business.
